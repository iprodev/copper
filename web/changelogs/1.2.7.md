some threads posts gave "couldn't find anything", even though there was a video in them. fetchr saves that video now.

- **videos attached to threads posts**: a threads post can be text with an instagram reel (or photo) attached under it. the video isn't part of the post itself, so fetchr didn't see it. now it does, also through share links (threads.com/share/...).
