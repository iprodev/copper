---
title: "links from many more sites"
date: "27 November, 2026"
---

fetchr now downloads from about 1800 more sites, like ted, bandcamp, imgur, 9gag, archive.org and odysee. paste a link from a site fetchr doesn't support itself, and it'll find the video, song or picture for you.

## other sites
- **with yt-dlp**: links from sites without fetchr's own support are looked up with [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp), which knows about 1800 sites. fetchr still downloads the files itself, so the quality you pick, audio only, muted videos and resuming downloads all work as usual.
- **best quality**: when a site has video and sound as separate files, they're joined into one, like on youtube.
- **albums**: albums and galleries with videos or pictures let you pick what to save.
- **always up to date**: sites change often, so the server gets the newest yt-dlp every day by itself.
- **safe**: links come from anyone, so yt-dlp runs on its own and can only reach the public internet, never the server's internal network.

## other fixes
- files without a known quality don't get empty brackets "()" in their name anymore.
