---
title: "a smarter telegram bot"
date: "22 November, 2026"
---

fetchr's telegram bot got a lot better: it speaks your language, lets you pick the quality of every download, and comes with a full admin panel for whoever runs it.

## for everyone using the bot
- **pick your language**: new users choose between persian and english, then get a welcome message and a keyboard with help, settings and language.
- **quality buttons**: after you send a link, pick 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p or audio only.
- **default quality**: set one in settings, and the bot won't ask every time.
- links are still never stored. the bot only keeps your telegram id, language and settings.

## for whoever runs the bot
- **admin panel** with `/admin`:
    - stats about users and downloads, and the most used services.
    - broadcasts to all users, with a preview before anything is sent.
    - required channels that users join before using the bot.
    - an editable welcome message for each language.
    - banning users, and a maintenance mode that only lets admins in.

## for whoever runs a fetchr server
- **automatic monitoring**: every 10 minutes the api, the warp connection, youtube downloads, the containers and the disk space are checked. common problems are fixed automatically, like a stuck api or warp, or youtube blocking warp's address. the bot's admins are told in telegram when something breaks or works again.
- docker images of every release are tagged with its version, so updating never replaces an older version.
