youtube videos saved in "max" quality (or 1440p and 2160p) failed, or only worked through yt-dlp. they're saved by fetchr itself again.

- **max quality on youtube**: for qualities above 1080p, fetchr asks youtube the way its website's embedded player does, which youtube stopped answering. when that fails now, the video is saved in the best quality youtube gives fetchr's usual way, instead of failing.
